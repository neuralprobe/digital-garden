# Knowledge Systems

Knowledge systems are not merely places to store information. They shape what
can be found, compared, revised, and eventually written.

This collection follows two connected questions:

1. How can small notes accumulate into substantial writing?
2. How can selected writing become a public garden without exposing the private
   notebook behind it?

The collection follows one editorial path:

```text
Private notes -> Reusable ideas -> Selected cluster -> Public garden
```

## Reading path

1. [Zettelkasten as a Writing Engine](/posts/zettelkasten-as-a-writing-engine/) explains the note types, links, and
   bottom-up workflow that turn reading into reusable thought.
2. [Building My Digital Garden](/posts/building-my-digital-garden/) shows how Obsidian, Hooni, Astro, and a
   relationship graph create a selective publication pipeline.
